Train within your control
  • Stop exercising with chest pain, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness or sharp pain. Severe or lasting chest pain: get emergency help.
  • Talk to a doctor first if you have an injury, pain, a heart or other health condition, have been inactive for a long time, or are under 18.
  • Only use equipment you can verify will hold your weight.
