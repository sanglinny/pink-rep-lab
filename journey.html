Pink Rep Lab starter plan (example, not a prescription)

  • Train two or three non-consecutive days a week.
  • Push: wall, incline or floor push-ups, controlled reps, stop when form breaks.
  • Pull: hang, row, band-assisted pull or slow lowering, only the ones you can control on equipment you can verify.
  • Rest until you can keep good form.
  • Stop with chest pain, unusual shortness of breath, dizziness or sharp pain. Severe or lasting chest pain: get emergency help.
