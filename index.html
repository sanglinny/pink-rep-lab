The Bar: Four Coaching Options Toward Your First Pull-Up

Why the bar feels impossible, four coaching options (hang with set shoulders, rows, band-assisted pulls, slow lowering), a starter template, and a way to track progress. These options have not been tested as a ladder in any trial.

Chapters
  • The goal and the muscles
  • Step 1: hang and set your shoulders
  • Step 2: rows
  • Step 3: band-assisted pulls
  • Step 4: slow lowering
  • Does the ladder work? (honest limits)
  • Starter template and sample session
  • When to move on
  • Staying safe
  • Record your baseline
Sources
  • Youdas et al. 2010, J Strength Cond Res (pull-up and chin-up EMG, 21 men and 4 women)
  • Dickie et al. 2017, J Electromyogr Kinesiol (pull-up variations, EMG)
  • Youdas et al. 2016, J Strength Cond Res (inverted row EMG)
  • Enes et al. 2025, J Strength Cond Res (repetition tempo meta-analysis)
