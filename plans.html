The push-up ladder

StepVersionNotes
1Wall push-upPalms flat at chest height, arms straight, one line from head to heel. Walk your feet back to make it harder.
2Incline push-upHands on a sturdy bench or table. The lower the surface, the harder it gets.
3Floor push-upHands just below shoulder level, feet together, gaze at the floor. About 2 seconds down, 1 second up, until elbows reach about 90 degrees.

Starter plan: 3 days a week (example)

2 to 3 sets of about 8 to 12 clean reps, using the hardest version you can do with good form. More than 12 easily? Move up one step. Cannot reach 8 clean reps? Move down one step.
