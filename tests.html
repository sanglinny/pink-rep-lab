Push-up starting guide

This is Pink Rep Lab's own simple starting guide, not a medical test or a validated strength assessment. The 8 to 12 rep range is our practice rule, not a scientific threshold.

Use controlled repetitions and stop when your form breaks, you feel pain, or you become unusually unwell. Do not force extra reps to reach a target. Wall, incline and floor push-ups have different difficulty, so compare your progress using the same variation and setup. Enter how many clean reps you did, then see a suggested starting step.
