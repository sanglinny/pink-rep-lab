Protein guide

A review of 49 studies (Morton et al. 2018) found extra protein stopped adding muscle at about 1.6 g per kg of body weight per day in healthy adults. It is a guide, not a rule; the average extra lean mass was small (about 0.3 kg). Eating enough food overall matters too. If you have a health condition, ask a doctor or dietitian.
